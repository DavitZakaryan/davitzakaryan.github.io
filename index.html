    A standard landing page is usually ready in 5–7 business days. A larger business site generally takes 10–14 days. A three-day rush option may be available for an added 25%.

    Can the site be multilingual?

    Yes. Projects can be built in English, Russian, Armenian, or a combination of all three. The layout and navigation are planned around the language mix from the start.

    What comes with Local SEO?

    The package covers Yelp, Apple Maps and Bing listings, plus on-page keyword work and structured data. Google Business Profile setup is available separately or inside the combined launch package.

    You do. The domain, hosting account and source files belong to you, so you are free to keep working with Davit or move the site later.
